당신은 미국 출신의 원어민 영어 번역가입니다.

아래 한국어 스크립트는 언어 학습 앱의 상황별 대화 레퍼런스야.
이걸 영어로 번역해줘.

규칙:
1. 대화 전체(AI turn + user turn 모두)를 영어로 작성
2. 한국어 레퍼런스에 이미 영어로 쓰인 줄은 그대로 유지
3. 자연스러운 구어체 사용 (앱에서 학습자가 실제로 쓸 표현)
4. 섹션 제목(### 1. 들어가자마자 등)은 한국어 유지
5. 파일 구조(##, ###) 그대로 유지

## 번역 결과
# 6. 리액션 모음 | 감정 표현, 맞장구, 감탄사

## 한국어만 모아보기

### 1. 친구 만나러 가는 길, 버스 정류장에서
A: Do you know when this bus is coming?
User: Huh? Sorry?
A: When is this bus coming?
User: Could you speak a little slower? I didn’t get it. I’m not a native speaker. What was that?
A: I said, when is this bus coming?
User: Ah, it just left.
A: Ugh, I should’ve taken that one.
User: Too bad.
A: Anyway, thanks.

### 2. 안부 묻기
A: How have you been lately?
User: Couldn’t be better. I’ve been playing guitar these days. I played all day yesterday.
A: That must’ve been fun.
User: Yeah, definitely. How about you?
A: Same as usual. <Same as always, you know.> I’m so tired these days.
User: I know, me too. Tell me about it.
Suddenly, an elderly woman sets down her heavy bags next to them.
User: I’ll help you. Are you okay?
Grandma: Thank you so much. You saved me!
They come back.
A: That’s very kind of you.
User: Sorry, where were we? Could you repeat the last part?
A: Ah, it’s nothing major, but I think I’ve been a bit tired lately.
User: Ah right, did something happen? Tell me.

A: I didn’t get the job.
User: That sucks. I can’t believe they didn’t recognize your talent!
A: Right? lol It is disappointing...
User: That must be tough, but it’s okay. You’ve got this.

A: Thanks for listening today. You made my day.
User: Don’t mention it. I should be the one thanking you. Thanks for sharing. Let’s meet on Saturday.
A: Sounds good. Want to grab a drink?
User: Ah, I can’t this time. How about next week instead? We could grab a drink then.
A: Sounds perfect. I’m all for it.

### 3. 이후 술집에서
User: I got a promotion!
A: Good for you! That’s awesome!
A: You deserve it. I knew you could do it.
User: Thanks. It still doesn’t feel real. It’s my first time taking a management role, so I’m a little scared.
A: That’s a big deal. Why are you doubting yourself? You’re good at this. You’ll do great.
User: Do you really think so?
A: Go for it. I’m rooting for you.
User: Thanks. Then I’ll try to be brave.
A: Good choice. I’ve got your back.
